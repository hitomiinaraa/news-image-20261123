A second attempt at one day, drawn from scratch after the first version was set aside.
The front page keeps four stories large and lets the rest of the day sit quietly below them.
Type was set big enough to read at a glance, with the date doing the work of a headline.
The rebuild traded density for breathing room, so no panel crowds its neighbour.
Colours stay flat and warm, chosen to read well on a dark screen as much as a light one.
Every story in the frame comes from the same Monday, nothing borrowed from another day.
What changed most was the order: the strongest story leads, the rest follow in size.
If it reads in three seconds, it did its job.
